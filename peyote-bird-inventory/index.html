Metals Inventory Declining

  • Metals down $10,478 (-8.7%) from $120,335 to $109,857 year-over-year
  • Silver is the primary driver — 139,880g on hand valued at $92,740 (84% of metals total)
  • Stone weights declining 15–25% across most categories — consistent drawdown without full replenishment
  • Three-year compounding: many stone categories now at 50–60% of their 2023 weight levels

Stones Value UP Despite Weight Declines

  • Cabs & Stones up $8,779 in dollar value — pricing adjustments offsetting weight declines
  • Stones ($130K) now exceed metals ($110K) — cabs are the larger asset class
  • Per-carat pricing structure allows value to hold even as quantities shrink
  • 77 active stone categories tracked with individual cost-per-carat rates

Critical Stockout Risks

  • Amber down 16% — 1,600g vs 1,905g prior year (updated: +750g added to count)
  • Moonstone down 43% — 2,600g vs 4,595g. High-demand stone, down 53% over 2 years
  • Garnet down 43% — 1,816g vs 3,175g. Needs immediate replenishment review
  • Asia Trip Turquoise down 40% — 1,543g vs 2,559g. Down 55% over 2 years
  • Turquoise Round down 40% — 2,360g vs 3,931g. Core product component
  • Carved Animals/Cameos down 56% — spiked in 2024, now correcting sharply

Missing, Excluded & Written Off

  • Chalcedony - Blue: MISSING — marked "missing" on count sheet. 2,993g in 2024. Needs physical location search
  • Write-offs per Mark Alexander (3.30.26): multiple items written off cost — Bone, Coral Red, Cubic Zirconia, and others
  • Pearls relocated: Pearl Cabs, Loose, and Strands "moved to pile" per Mark Alexander
  • Coral (Apple Sponge, Bamboo): Zero count — combined 3,582g in 2023, showing zero in 2024

Positive Signals

  • Turquoise Singles to Pair up 20% — 10,105g vs 8,443g. Strong pairing stock for production
  • Mixed Turquoise Singles up 12.5% — healthy inventory level maintained
  • Labradorite up 6.4% — 3,207g, well-stocked and growing
  • Turquoise Triangle/Diamond: new category added for 2025
  • Silver market upside: book value ($0.663/g) vs spot ($2.41/g) = $337K potential — 3.6× book
