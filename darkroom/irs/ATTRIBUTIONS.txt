Impulse Response Attributions
==============================

The 6 real-room impulse responses in this directory are derived from the
OpenAIR archive (https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/), maintained by the
Audio Lab at the University of York. Each source IR is licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Each file in this directory has been MODIFIED from the source as follows:
  - downmixed to mono (where source was multichannel)
  - resampled to 48 kHz (where source was 96 kHz or 44.1 kHz)
  - converted to 16-bit PCM
  - truncated to a target length appropriate for the slot's musical use
  - cosine-tapered fade-out applied over the trailing 30–45% of the file
  - peak-normalized to approximately −0.5 dBFS

Source spaces, in slot order:

  01-wood.wav       → Falkland Palace Royal Tennis Court
                       (Source: falkland_tennis_court_omni.wav, mono 48k)
                       https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/?page_id=476

  04-tape.wav       → Stairway, University of York
                       (Source: stairwell_ortf.wav, stereo ORTF 96k → mono mix)
                       https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/?page_id=678

  05-hall.wav       → Jack Lyons Concert Hall, University of York
                       (Source: rir_jack_lyons_lp2_96k.wav, B-format 4ch 96k → W only)
                       https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/?page_id=571

  06-chamber.wav    → Hamilton Mausoleum
                       (Source: hm2_000_wx_48k.wav, W omnidirectional 48k)
                       https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/?page_id=502

  07-cathedral.wav  → St. Paul's Cathedral
                       (Source: mono_P6T1_wg_away_441.wav, mono 44.1k)
                       https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/?page_id=1516

  08-cave.wav       → Maes Howe (neolithic chambered cairn)
                       (Source: mh3_000_wx_48k.wav, W omnidirectional 48k)
                       https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/?page_id=602

Slots 02 (plate) and 03 (spring) remain procedurally synthesized inside
darkroom.html — they emulate gear, not rooms, and do not require IR data.
