# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences for jacksonalexanderstudio.com: (1) Jackson Alexander himself — artist/designer/producer in Santa Fe, controlling his physical studio (Philips Hue lamps, music gear) through pages that double as tools; (2) portfolio visitors — potential clients, collaborators, and curious peers browsing his work, including the Playground section of coded experiments and generative pieces.

### Brand Personality
Refined, technical, quietly witty. An artist's studio that happens to write code — editorial gallery presentation with a mono/terminal undercurrent. Confident restraint over spectacle; numbered specimens over marketing cards.

### Aesthetic Direction
Established site system (match it, don't reinvent): near-black `#0c0c0c` headers and dark fields, white content areas, accent `#D95740` (terracotta red), hairline borders `#EBEBEB`. Type: Fragment Mono (body/labels), DM Sans (UI labels, uppercase letter-spaced), Cormorant Garamond italic (display headings), Pixelify Sans (big numeric specimens). Playground pieces are numbered tiles in a grid with sticky sidebar navigation. Interactive pages may go full-dark when the content is luminous (light art, visualizers).

### Design Principles
1. Match the house style precisely — new pages should read as chapters of the same book.
2. Tools and art are the same object: a control surface should be beautiful enough to show visitors.
3. Local-first honesty: public visitors get simulations; real hardware control only appears when the local daemon is reachable. Never fake capability.
4. Restraint in motion — slow, fluid, purposeful; no strobes, no bounce.
5. The API key and bridge details never ship in public HTML.
