CASE STUDY 04: JERICHO RIDGE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Tagline:
A hand-built dry-stacked stone structure on a mountain ridge outside Santa Fe — raw materials, simple tools, and a long view.
Project: Jericho Ridge
Discipline: Stone Fabrication, Land Work
Year: 2025
THE BRIEF:


EDIT -
A ridgeline at 8,000 feet with a 360-degree view of the Sangre de Cristos and the Rio Grande valley. The project was simple in concept — build a dry-stacked stone wind shelter using only the material already provided by the land itself. No mortar, no concrete, no power tools. Just a pickaxe, gloves, and the rocks underfoot. Inspired heavily by the land art of Andy Goldsworthy, this project was the first in what would become a series of rock walls across the property. 


PROCESS CAPTIONS:
- Wall detail — dry-stacked stone with salvaged timber
- Terrace taking shape — pickaxe and hand-placed stone
- Walls rising — last light on the ridge
- Fall on the ridge — wall and autumn color
- Tools of the trade — rake and pickaxe on the wall face


TAKEAWAY:
Jericho Ridge was my first attempt to break away from the type of work I had been confined to for a decade or more. Sitting at a desk inside and moving things across a screen day in day out.  With this project, I was now just hauling rocks up and down a hill and stacking them up one at a time. It ended up being one of my favorite projects to this day: quiet work in nature, building something physical with my hands, and entering the flow state that is so hard to truly sink into these days.

CASE STUDY 05: ZOZOBRA: 100 YEARS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Tagline:
Poster design for Zozobra: 100 Years of Fire and Redemption — a documentary celebrating a century of Santa Fe's most iconic tradition.
Client: Hutton Films
Discipline: Poster Design, Film Branding
Year: 2024
THE BRIEF:
Every September, Santa Fe burns a 50-foot marionette called Zozobra — a tradition started by artist Will Shuster in 1924. For the centennial, Hutton Films produced a short documentary and needed a poster that captured the weight and weirdness of a hundred years of burning gloom. The ask was a single image that could work on screen, in print, and on the marquee at Violet Crown Cinema for the premiere.
PROCESS CAPTIONS:
- Archival reference — early Zozobra, Will Shuster era
- Modern Zozobra — fireworks and fire at Fiestas de Santa Fe
TAKEAWAY:


EDIT- 
The final black-and-white composition balanced archival tone with contemporary clarity. Displayed across Santa Fe for the premiere, the poster functioned as both promotional material and commemorative artifact.

CASE STUDY 06: 1974 BMW 2002tii
----------------------------------------------------------------
Tagline:
Full restoration — engine, suspension, bodywork, seats, stereo, wheels, wiring, interior
Client: Personal
Discipline: Restoration, Fabrication, Mechanical
Year: 2021 – Present


THE Project 


A 1974 BMW 2002tii — mechanically tired, electrically unreliable, and sunfaded after fifty years of wear and tear. The end goal was to bring the car back to life as a daily driver: rebuild the engine, upgrade the suspension, and restore the finer details back to their former glory. 


PROCESS CAPTIONS:
- Engine tear-down — head pulled, block exposed, full top-end rebuild
- Reassembled — M10 back together, Original BMW Teile
- Before — fifty years of UV on original Verona Red
- After — cut, polish, and seal on the original paint
- Finished console — VDO gauges, Blaupunkt Frankfurt, BMW shift knob
- Restored interior — black sport seats, new floor mats, full cabin detail


TAKEAWAY:
This project was a collaboration between my father and me. Though painfully slow-moving, expensive, and frustrating at times, we both came out of the experience with a deeper appreciation for the car, inside and out. It is safe to say the car will be in the family for years to come.

CASE STUDY 07: ALL EYES ON US
----------------------------------------------------------------
Tagline:
A hand-made, mixed-media zine exploring surveillance, privacy, and the architecture of observation — built from newspaper clippings, drawings, satellite imagery, redacted documents, vintage graph paper, 


Client: Academic
Discipline: Editorial, Collage, Typography
Year: 2017
THE BRIEF:
Create a self-published zine as a final project for a college design course. The result was a 13-page hand-assembled exploration of mass surveillance, data collection, and the feeling of being watched. Every page was built physically: cut, torn, taped, redacted, and scanned by hand. 


TAKEAWAY:
Building every page by hand — sourcing material from newspapers, redacting text, punching holes, layering tape — forced a slower, more deliberate process than the usual screen-based workflows. It re-instilled my love for collage and analog methods of creating that I still work with almost 10 years later. 


Made in collaboration with Ian McMullen

CASE STUDY 08: FLASH SHEETS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Tagline:
The flash sheets serve as an ever-growing archive, the result of revisiting a lifetime of drawings and doodles and assembling them into a digital collection. The process helps me understand the scope of my work, allowing me to see recurring patterns and themes, build upon past projects and ideas, and weave a clearer thread through my creative process.


Project: Flash Sheets
Discipline: Illustration, Icon Design, Ink Drawing
Year: 2023 – Present
THE BRIEF:
A growing collection of characters that started filling up sketchbooks and never stopped. Faces, figures, totems, creatures, glyphs, geometric forms — all drawn by hand in ink, then scanned and arranged into flash sheets. 


TAKEAWAY:
The flash sheets are the throughline across most of my work — the visual DNA that shows up in logo work, in the Rocket Ramps icons, in fabrication details, in the logos, etc. Drawing every day without a brief turned out to be the most useful practice of all. The archive, similarly to the garden, just keeps growing.
